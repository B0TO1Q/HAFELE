FAQ

Common questions.

How do I get a quote?

Use the quote form on the home page, or text (203) 560-6079 with your address and 2–4 photos of the area. Pricing is based on your property, so photos help us quote accurately.

How fast will I hear back?

Within 24 hours.

Do I pick a mowing day?

No. Your property is added to our route schedule, weekly or every two weeks, and we keep it on a regular rhythm.

Which towns do you serve?

Avon, Bristol, Burlington, Canton, Collinsville, Farmington, Harwinton, Litchfield, New Hartford, Northfield, Plymouth, Terryville, Thomaston, Torrington and Unionville. Nearby and not listed? Ask.

Do you plow commercial lots?

Yes. We plow residential driveways and commercial lots, for the whole season or per storm.

How can I pay?

Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, Apple Pay, Jobber, cash or check.
