FAQ

Common questions.

What is Four-Season Care?

One annual agreement covering your property all year: spring and fall cleanups, mowing and upkeep through the growing season, and plowing all winter. We walk the property with you first and set the scope and price together.

How do I request an estimate?

Use the estimate form on the home page, or text (203) 560-6079 with your address and 2–4 photos of the area. Pricing is based on your property, so photos help us price it accurately.

How fast will I hear back?

Within 24 hours.

Do I pick a mowing day?

No. Your property is added to our route schedule, weekly or every two weeks, and we keep it on a regular rhythm.

Which towns do you serve?

Avon, Bristol, Burlington, Canton, Collinsville, Farmington, Harwinton, Litchfield, New Hartford, Northfield, Plymouth, Terryville, Thomaston, Torrington and Unionville. Nearby and not listed? Ask.

Do you plow commercial lots?

Yes. We plow residential driveways and commercial lots, for the whole season or per storm.

How can I pay?

Check, Apple Pay, PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, Jobber or cash.
